Frequently Asked Questions & Global Policies

Everything you need to know about international shipping, customs duty prepayments (DDP), seller onboarding standards, and multi-currency escrow protection.

How are customs duties and import taxes calculated?

OmniTrade uses Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) logistics protocols. During checkout, import taxes and VAT are calculated using official tariff classifications based on your destination country. You will never be asked to pay unexpected carrier fees at your doorstep.

What is the 30-Day International Return Guarantee?

If an item arrives damaged or materially differs from its specifications, our global escrow guarantee refunds 100% of your product cost, original shipping, and prepaid duties. We provide pre-paid return labels to localized consolidation centers in North America and Western Europe.

How do independent international sellers join OmniTrade?

Sellers must undergo physical workshop or laboratory verification, submit certified corporate business registration, and maintain a minimum 4.80 customer satisfaction rating. Platform commission is capped at 8.5% with bi-weekly automated payouts in local currency (JPY, EUR, GBP, USD).

How does the Multi-Currency Escrow Payment Protection work?

When an order is placed, buyer funds are held in secure cross-border escrow. Settlement is only disbursed to international makers once carrier telemetry confirms safe delivery and inspection. Both buyers and vendors are protected against currency volatility and international fraud.
